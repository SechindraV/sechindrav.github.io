## Research Focus
I am seeking motivated graduate students to join my research group on agricultural and environmental sustainability. Students will have flexibility to define specific research questions within broad themes such as:

- Climate adaptation in agriculture and water resources  
- Governance of energy transitions and sustainable infrastructure  
- Environmental policy and institutional responses to climate risks  
- Intersections of climate justice, sustainability, and policy design

Students will have the opportunity to design independent research projects and collaborate across disciplines.

<details>
<summary><strong>Program Environment</strong></summary>

UGA’s Integrative Conservation Ph.D. Program (ICON) trains students to connect ecological, social, and policy dimensions of global environmental challenges. Students will work within a vibrant, interdisciplinary network at UGA, including the River Basin Center, and will have the opportunity to pursue collaborations across ecology, economics, anthropology, geography, engineering, and public policy.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Qualifications</strong></summary>

**Ph.D. applicants:**
- Meet UGA Graduate School admission requirements  
- Master’s degree in environmental studies, geography, economics, public policy, or related discipline  
- Background or interest in environmental governance, adaptation, or sustainability  
- Strong writing skills; quantitative research experience preferred

**Master’s applicants:**
- Bachelor’s degree in environmental studies, policy, geography, or related field  
- Interest in environmental governance, adaptation, or sustainability  
- Research or policy experience preferred but not required

Applicants from historically underserved groups (e.g., Black, Indigenous, and People of Color, LGBTQ+, women, first-generation students) are strongly encouraged to apply.

</details>
